HV Battery Isolation Fault

Explicação diagnóstica e técnica
Code P0AA6 sets when the HV battery isolation monitoring circuit detects a resistance path between the high-voltage bus and chassis ground that is lower than the safe threshold, or the isolation-monitoring circuit itself fails its self-test. The same monitor can report this fault specifically while an external EVSE is connected and charging, if the leakage path traced back is on the charger side of the bus rather than the pack or drive side. The most common physical causes are moisture intrusion into a HV connector or the battery pack seal, a damaged HV cable jacket rubbing against a chassis ground point, and a failed isolation-monitor module.

Reductant Valve Circuit Performance

Explicação diagnóstica e técnica
P202F (Reductant/Regeneration Supply Control Circuit Range/Performance) covers the solenoid valves that purge residual reductant from the dosing line after injection or regulate the compressed-air assist supply, protecting the system from urea crystallizing inside the lines when the vehicle shuts off. A valve that will not open leaves DEF standing in the line to crystallize on the next cold soak, while a valve stuck open bleeds system air pressure; both conditions and the underlying circuit-open/short fault share the same electrical diagnosis and valve-replacement repair.

Estes 22 códigos de diagnóstico compartilham um mesmo mecanismo de detecção e um mesmo caminho de reparo; por isso o MechanicDB os agrupa em uma única família de falhas redigida, em vez de repetir a mesma explicação 22 vezes.

Código P26DA: Engine Coolant Pump Clutch Control Circuit High

Explicación diagnóstica y técnica
Code P26DA (Engine Coolant Pump Clutch Control Circuit High) sets when the ECM detects an open circuit, out-of-range voltage, or a stuck-on/stuck-off condition while controlling the electro-viscous or electromagnetic clutch on a belt-driven coolant pump, which decouples the pump at cold start to speed engine warm-up. Causes are a worn clutch coil, silicone fluid leakage from an electro-viscous unit, or damaged control wiring along the front accessory drive.

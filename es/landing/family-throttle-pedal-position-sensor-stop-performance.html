Throttle Pedal Position Sensor Stop Performance

Explicación diagnóstica y técnica
P2109 (Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor A Minimum Stop Performance) fires when one of the redundant throttle or accelerator pedal position sensor channels does not read the expected voltage at its mechanical end-of-travel stop during the ECM's self-calibration. A pedal or throttle stop that has worn or shifted out of factory position, a sensor track that has drifted with age, and a battery disconnect that cleared a learned calibration without the platform's relearn procedure being performed are the common causes.

Estos 12 códigos de diagnóstico comparten un mismo mecanismo de detección y una misma vía de reparación; por eso MechanicDB los agrupa en una única familia de fallas redactada, en lugar de repetir la misma explicación 12 veces.

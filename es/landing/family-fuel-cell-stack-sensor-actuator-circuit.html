Fuel Cell Stack Sensor Actuator Circuit

Explicación diagnóstica y técnica
U3549 covers the hydrogen storage, stack, and balance-of-plant sensors and valves found on a fuel-cell electric vehicle -- tank pressure/temperature sensors, anode/cathode valves, and stack coolant sensors -- none of which exist on a conventional gasoline, diesel, or battery-EV platform. Because these circuits sit on pressurized hydrogen lines and a high-voltage stack, diagnosis and repair require fuel-cell-specific technician training, hydrogen leak-detection equipment, and the manufacturer's lockout procedure rather than general shop tools. A failed sensor, a stuck valve, or corrosion at a stack coolant connector are the most frequent causes.

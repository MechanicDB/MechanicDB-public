Drive Motor Position Sensor Circuit

Explicación diagnóstica y técnica
Code P0A3F sets when the control module detects an implausible signal from the drive motor's resolver, which tracks rotor position by exciting a coil and comparing the returned sine/cosine envelope — the electrical angle it reports disagrees with the motor's other measured behavior. Because the resolver and its excitation/sine/cosine leads route through a conduit inside the motor housing, common causes are a corroded connector at the point the leads exit the housing, chafed conductors inside that conduit, and physical resolver drift from a bent rotor air gap after a prior service procedure that disturbed the motor's internals. This circuit fault should be distinguished from the separate resolver learning-routine fault, which occurs even with healthy resolver hardware if the electrical-to-mechanical offset was never correctly relearned.

Hv Battery Pack Coolant Valve

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0CDF sets when the control module commands the HV battery pack's liquid-coolant control valve to a position and the feedback signal or the pack's resulting coolant-loop temperature does not match the commanded state. The most common physical causes are debris or scale seizing the valve body where it sits in the pack's coolant manifold, a corroded actuator connector, and an internally worn valve motor that loses commanded-position accuracy over time. Because this valve directs coolant flow to or around the pack rather than the cabin or power-electronics loops, a stuck-closed valve can produce rapid pack-temperature codes even while the rest of the vehicle's cooling system tests normal.

These 23 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 23 times.

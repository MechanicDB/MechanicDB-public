Fuel Injector Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P00BB (Fuel Injector Insufficient Flow - Forced Limited Power) sets when the ECM detects an open, shorted, or out-of-range electrical condition while driving a specific cylinder's fuel injector. Causes are a failed injector coil, a corroded or unseated injector connector, or damaged wiring in the injector harness.

These 155 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 155 times.

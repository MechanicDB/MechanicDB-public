Control Module Power Ignition Input

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
U3000 (Control Module) is set internally by a control module that monitors its own supply rail, ground return, or an ignition-derived input (accessory, run, start, or a disable/enable line) and finds the circuit out of range, open, or miscorrelated with another related input. Unlike a network wiring fault, this family points at the module's own power path -- the fuse, ignition relay, splice pack, or ground eyelet feeding that specific module -- rather than the communication bus itself. A blown fuse, a corroded ground eyelet under a bolt, or a worn ignition switch contact are the most common physical causes.

These 57 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 57 times.

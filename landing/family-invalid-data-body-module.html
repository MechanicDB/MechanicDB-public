Invalid Data Body Module

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
U0422 means a body-network module received a message from the BCM, gateway, or a related body node with a value flagged as implausible -- for example a door-ajar or switch-input signal stuck in an impossible state -- while the network link itself remains healthy. Because the BCM often relays sensor and switch inputs gathered from many small body sensors, invalid data here frequently traces to one specific stuck switch, corroded switch-input connector, or a mis-set configuration option rather than the BCM hardware itself.

These 24 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 24 times.

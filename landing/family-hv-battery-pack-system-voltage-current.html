HV Battery Pack System Voltage Current

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0AF8 sets when the pack's system-level voltage/current monitor — the aggregate reading across all modules combined, rather than any single module — falls outside the range the battery management system expects, or swings faster than is physically plausible for the whole pack. The most common physical causes are a systemic cooling shortfall driving accelerated aging across multiple modules at once, a loose main HV bus-bar connection between the pack and the junction box, and a marginal 12V accessory-battery supply feeding the BMS. Because this check evaluates the pack as a system, it can flag even when every individual module's own voltage-fault check passes.

These 16 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 16 times.

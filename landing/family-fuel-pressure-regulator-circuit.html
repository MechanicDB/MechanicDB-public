Fuel Pressure Regulator Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0089 (Fuel Pressure Regulator A Performance) sets when the ECM detects an open circuit, out-of-range voltage, or a performance mismatch while driving the fuel pressure regulator solenoid. Causes include a failed regulator coil, a mechanically stuck metering valve from fuel contamination, or damaged wiring or connector pins at the regulator.

These 39 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 39 times.

Fault Family System: Powertrain (P-Codes) 131 SAE Codes

HV Battery Voltage Sense

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0B3B sets when the battery management system detects an implausible reading from one cell-group's voltage-sense tap on the pack's cell-monitoring board — the value disagrees with its neighboring cell groups or fails a hard-limit check. Because each tap is a dedicated sense wire soldered or crimped to a module busbar rather than a standalone sensor, the most common causes are a sense-wire connector that backed out at the cell-monitoring board, a crimp that corroded at the busbar tap, and a monitoring-IC input channel that failed on the board itself. This circuit differs from the pack-level voltage-sense family in that it reports one cell group's voltage rather than the summed pack voltage.

These 131 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 131 times.

Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0544 (Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor Circuit Bank 1 Sensor 1) sets when the ECM sees an open, shorted, or implausible resistance from an exhaust gas temperature sensor used to protect the turbo, catalyst, or DPF from thermal damage and to manage regeneration events. Causes are a sensor tip fouled or degraded by exhaust soot, a corroded connector, or wiring damaged by exhaust heat.

These 62 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 62 times.

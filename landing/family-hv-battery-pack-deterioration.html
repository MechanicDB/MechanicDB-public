HV Battery Pack Deterioration

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0A7E sets when the battery management system's pack-deterioration (state-of-health) model determines the HV battery pack's usable capacity, internal resistance, or maximum temperature has moved outside the acceptable range for continued safe operation. The most common physical causes are ordinary calendar/cycle aging of the battery chemistry, a persistent cooling-system shortfall that has driven repeated thermal cycling, and one or more modules that degraded faster than the rest of the pack, creating a capacity or impedance mismatch the balancing routine can no longer correct.

These 30 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 30 times.

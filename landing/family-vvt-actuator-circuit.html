VVT Actuator Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0010 (A Camshaft Position Actuator A Control Circuit/Open Bank 1) sets when the ECM detects an open circuit or out-of-range voltage while driving a camshaft phaser (VVT) control solenoid. The usual causes are a failed OCV solenoid coil, a corroded or unseated harness connector at the valve cover, or chafed wiring between the ECM and the actuator. Codes naming the phaser's own actuator position sensor (as opposed to the primary crank-referenced camshaft position sensor) group here too, since the sensor shares the same connector and wiring diagnosis path as the OCV solenoid, as do codes for the actuator's integrated temperature-sensor circuit, which is diagnosed through that same connector and harness.

These 129 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 129 times.

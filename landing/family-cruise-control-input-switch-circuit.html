Cruise Control Input Switch Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0564 (Cruise Control Multi-Function Input A Circuit) sets when the ECM detects an implausible voltage, a stuck condition, or a correlation mismatch from one of the driver-operated cruise control switches (on/off/set/resume/coast/accelerate/cancel/distance, or the multi-function input pod) or determines the system cannot safely engage because vehicle speed is too high. Causes are worn or contaminated switch contacts inside the steering-wheel pod, a chafed clockspring/wiring connection, or a multi-function resistor-ladder switch drifting out of its voltage window.

These 24 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 24 times.

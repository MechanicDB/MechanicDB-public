Throttle Position Sensor Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0120 (Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor/Switch A Circuit) sets when the ECM detects an open, shorted, or out-of-range voltage, or an implausible rate of change, from a throttle body or accelerator pedal position sensor channel. Causes are a worn sensor track, a corroded connector, or chafed wiring between the sensor and the ECM.

These 41 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 41 times.

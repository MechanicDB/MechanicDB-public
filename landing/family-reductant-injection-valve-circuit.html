Reductant Injection Valve Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
P201A (Reductant Injection Valve Circuit Range/Performance Bank 2 Unit 1) is set by the SCR dosing module when a specific reductant injector unit fails to draw its expected coil current on command, whether from an open winding, a shorted driver, or a valve mechanically stuck. Because the dosing valve lives directly in the hot, urea-crystal-prone exhaust stream, the most frequent physical cause is crystallized urea seizing the pintle, followed by a corroded connector at the dosing module and, less often, a burned-out injector coil.

These 21 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 21 times.

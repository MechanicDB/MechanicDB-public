Code P2529: Vacuum Reservoir Pressure Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P2529 (Vacuum Reservoir Pressure Sensor Circuit Intermittent) sets when the ECM sees an open, shorted, or implausible signal from the pressure sensor monitoring the vacuum reservoir that supplies actuators such as the brake booster or emissions valves. Causes are a corroded sensor connector, a cracked sensor housing losing its vacuum seal, or wiring chafed near the reservoir's engine-bay mounting location.

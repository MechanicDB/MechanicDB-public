O2 Pumping Current Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P2237 (O2 Sensor Positive Current Control Circuit/Open Bank 1 Sensor 1) belongs to the wideband (air-fuel ratio) sensor's pumping cell, whose positive and negative current the ECM drives to hold the sensing chamber at stoichiometry, reading that current as the actual air-fuel ratio. Faults in the pumping current control or trim circuits are set when the current is uncontrollable, out of range, or its calibration trim resistor reads wrong; a cracked sensor element from thermal shock or water splash, and damage to the sensor's dedicated wiring legs, dominate the causes.

These 47 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 47 times.

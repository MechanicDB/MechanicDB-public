Cylinder Deactivation Intake Valve Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P3401 (Cylinder 1 Deactivation/Intake Valve Control Circuit/Open) reports an electrical fault - open circuit, out-of-range voltage, or a performance deviation - on the intake-side oil-control solenoid that collapses a named cylinder's intake valve for fuel-saving cylinder deactivation, whether logged per cylinder or as a bank-wide summary. A failed solenoid coil, an intake-side oil-control screen starved of switching pressure by sludge, or damaged wiring at the lifter-oil-manifold connector are the usual physical culprits.

These 52 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 52 times.

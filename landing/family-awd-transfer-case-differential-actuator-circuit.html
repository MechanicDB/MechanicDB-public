Awd Transfer Case Differential Actuator Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code C0071 (2/4 Wheel Drive Status Input) sets when the driveline control module cannot verify correct operation of a power-transfer unit, transfer case, or rear differential actuator -- the actuator's position sensor disagrees with commanded position, a temperature sensor reads out of range, the driveline module's own driver circuit fails, or a status input like the 2WD/4WD switch reports an implausible state. These electronically controlled units engage AWD/4WD by moving an internal clutch pack or range fork with a motor or solenoid actuator, so most faults trace to a failed actuator motor, a worn position sensor, low or contaminated differential fluid causing the actuator to bind, or a corroded connector on the underbody driveline harness. A vehicle stuck in one drive mode, a driveline clunk on engagement, or a warning message are the typical driver-facing symptoms. This family also covers codes naming the actuator's integrated position or temperature sensor circuit directly -- including the power-transfer unit and rear differential temperature sensor codes (C0093, C0094) -- since those sensors share the same connector and wiring diagnosis path as the actuator itself.

These 40 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 40 times.

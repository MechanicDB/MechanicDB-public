Code P032F: Combustion Engine Producing Insufficent Torque

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P032F (Combustion Engine Producing Insufficent Torque) sets when the ECM's torque management model calculates that the engine is producing significantly less torque than the driver-demanded or commanded value, based on cross-checking throttle position, airflow, fuel delivery, and ignition timing against a torque estimate. Because this is a modeled comparison rather than a single-sensor fault, causes span a vacuum/intake leak reducing available airflow, retarded ignition timing from a knock or cam-timing fault, restricted fuel delivery, or an underlying mechanical issue such as low compression.

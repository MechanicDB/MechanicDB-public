Cylinder Deactivation Exhaust Valve Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P3405 (Cylinder 1 Exhaust Valve Control Circuit/Open) sets when the ECM detects an open circuit, out-of-range voltage, or a performance deviation while driving the oil-control solenoid that collapses a specific cylinder's exhaust valve for cylinder deactivation, reported per cylinder or as a bank-wide aggregate. Because exhaust-side lifters run hotter and see more combustion byproduct than intake-side units, the leading causes are carbon and varnish fouling the oil-control screen, a heat-degraded solenoid coil, or damaged control wiring near the exhaust side of the valve cover.

These 52 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 52 times.

Turbo Boost Control Circuit

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P003A (Turbocharger/Supercharger Boost Control A Position Exceeded Learning Limit) sets when the ECM detects an electrical fault, an out-of-range learned position, or an overspeed condition while controlling the wastegate/boost control solenoid or actuator. Physical causes include a failed boost control solenoid, a seized or disconnected wastegate actuator rod, a stuck wastegate flap, or damaged control wiring.

These 59 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 59 times.

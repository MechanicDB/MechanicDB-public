Hv Battery Temperature Sensor

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
Code P0A9B sets when the battery management system compares one cell-group thermistor in the pack's thermistor string against its neighboring cell-group readings and the coolant-inlet reference, and finds a value that disagrees by more than the allowed spread. Each thermistor is threaded through the module stack on a shared ribbon harness, so the most common causes are a connector pin at the module-level junction that backed out or corroded, a thermistor bead that delaminated from the cell-group busbar it monitors, and — because these readings feed the BMS's current-derating logic — a genuinely hot module whose cooling contact has degraded rather than a sensor fault at all. Cell-median plausibility checks are what distinguish a single bad sensor from real thermal spread across the pack.

These 88 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 88 times.

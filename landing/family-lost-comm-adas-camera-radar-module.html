Lost Comm ADAS Camera Radar Module

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
U011F indicates a forward or rear camera, radar unit, or other driver-assistance sensor-fusion module has dropped off its dedicated high-speed CAN or Ethernet-based ADAS network. These modules mount rigidly to the windshield, fascia, or grille where they see direct impact risk from minor collisions and road debris, so a cracked mounting bracket or a damaged connector from a prior bumper repair is a common trigger alongside ordinary wiring faults. Any time the module is disconnected, repaired, or replaced, a static or dynamic calibration is required afterward or the system will not re-enable even once communication is restored.

These 42 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 42 times.

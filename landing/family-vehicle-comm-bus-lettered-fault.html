Vehicle Comm Bus Lettered Fault

Diagnostic & Technical Explanation
U0028 covers the SAE-generic Vehicle Communication Bus letters A through J that individual manufacturers assign to whichever CAN or serial segment they wire into a given platform, so the letter itself carries no fixed meaning -- the diagnosis is the same twisted-pair wiring check regardless of which letter is reported. These codes flag a specific electrical condition on that segment: a fully open bus, a marginal performance fault, or a wire pinned high, low, open, or shorted to its counterpart conductor. Because the letter only identifies which OEM-defined segment failed, the repair path always starts with locating that segment in the platform-specific wiring diagram before testing.

These 76 diagnostic trouble codes share one detection mechanism and one repair path, which is why MechanicDB groups them into a single authored fault family rather than repeating the same explanation 76 times.

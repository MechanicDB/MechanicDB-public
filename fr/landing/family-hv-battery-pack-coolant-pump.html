HV Battery Pack Coolant Pump

Diagnostic et explication technique
Code P0C32 sets when the control module commands the HV battery pack's dedicated coolant pump on or off and does not see the matching current draw, tachometer feedback, or pack-temperature response within the diagnostic window. The most common physical causes are a blown fuse or failed relay feeding this pump specifically, separate from the drive-motor or power-electronics pumps, impeller wear or cavitation from age, and a corroded low-current connector at the pump. Because the pack has its own coolant loop on most liquid-cooled platforms, this fault should not be diagnosed by servicing the drive-motor or power-electronics cooling pumps.

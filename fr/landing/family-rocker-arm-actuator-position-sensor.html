Rocker Arm Actuator Position Sensor

Diagnostic et explication technique
Code P264A (A Rocker Arm Actuator Position Sensor Circuit Bank 1) sets when the ECM sees an open, shorted, implausible, or intermittent signal from the position sensor confirming a rocker arm actuator actually switched lift states or collapsed its rockers as commanded. Because the sensor confirms mechanical motion driven by oil pressure, an implausible reading can equally mean a healthy sensor watching a hydraulically stuck actuator, so oil pressure and condition must be verified before replacing the sensor.

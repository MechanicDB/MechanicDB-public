Drive Motor Inverter Temp Sensor

Diagnostic et explication technique
Code P0AED sets when the control module detects an implausible reading from the NTC thermistor bonded to the drive motor inverter's IGBT (or SiC MOSFET) power-module baseplate — the reading disagrees with the commanded switching load or drifts outside the sensor's calibrated curve. The most common physical causes are degraded thermal-interface paste between the power module and its cold plate, which raises the true junction temperature well above what the coolant loop shows, a restricted or air-locked coolant passage specific to the inverter's cold plate, and a corroded NTC lead connector on the low-voltage control board. Because this sensor feeds the derate-then-shutdown ladder that protects the switching devices, a stuck-low reading can mask a real overtemperature condition and should be cross-checked against coolant-loop temperature before the sensor is condemned.

Ces 40 codes défaut de diagnostic partagent un même mécanisme de détection et une même voie de réparation ; c'est pourquoi MechanicDB les regroupe dans une seule famille de défauts rédigée, plutôt que de répéter la même explication 40 fois.

SRS Seatbelt Buckle Tension Sensor Circuit

Diagnostic et explication technique
Code B0050 (Driver Seat Belt Sensor) sets when the SRS module cannot read a plausible signal from a seat belt buckle switch or the belt tension sensor at a given seating position -- the switch stays latched-open or latched-closed regardless of actual buckle state, or the tension sensor's signal falls outside its calibrated range. This information feeds the seat belt reminder chime, the occupant classification system, and the airbag deployment algorithm, since a properly belted occupant may receive a different deployment strategy than an unbelted one. Typical causes are a worn buckle switch contact, a connector at the base of the seat exposed to repeated flexing, or a tension sensor damaged by a prior full seat-track travel.

Ces 11 codes défaut de diagnostic partagent un même mécanisme de détection et une même voie de réparation ; c'est pourquoi MechanicDB les regroupe dans une seule famille de défauts rédigée, plutôt que de répéter la même explication 11 fois.

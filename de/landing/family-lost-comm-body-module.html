Lost Comm Body Module

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
U013F (Lost Communication With Pedestrian Alert Control Module) means another module can no longer see the body control module, serial data gateway, security/immobilizer module, or a similar cabin-electrical hub on the body CAN network. These modules typically live behind a kick panel or under the dash and their connectors are exposed to condensation from HVAC ducting and to fretting corrosion from repeated trim removal during other service, rather than the engine-bay heat that affects powertrain nodes. Because the BCM/gateway often bridges multiple bus segments, a single failed connector here can look like several unrelated modules going offline at once.

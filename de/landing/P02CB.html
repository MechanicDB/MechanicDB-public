Code P02CB: Turbocharger/Supercharger B Underboost Condition

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code P02CB (Turbocharger/Supercharger B Underboost Condition) sets when boost pressure falls short of the commanded target, indicating a real mechanical underboost event rather than a sensor circuit fault. Causes are a boost leak in the charge piping, a wastegate stuck open, a worn turbocharger, or a restricted air filter.

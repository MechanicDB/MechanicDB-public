CAN Bus Off Fault

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
U0073 means a CAN controller has counted enough consecutive transmit errors to trip its bus-off protection and disconnect itself from the network entirely -- a controller-state fault, not a simple wiring open -- and it is almost always triggered by one specific module flooding the bus with corrupt frames or holding a line dominant. Because a healthy module can appear to be at fault just for reporting bus-off after another module misbehaves, the repair path is a half-split isolation test (unplug modules one at a time and watch for the bus to recover) rather than a blind wiring check. A dead-shorted CAN wire or a module stuck transmitting during a software hang are the two most common root causes.

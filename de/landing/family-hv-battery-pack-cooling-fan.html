HV Battery Pack Cooling Fan

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code P0A81 sets when the control module commands the HV battery pack's cooling fan on or off — used on air-cooled pack designs to pull cabin or ambient air through the pack — and does not see the matching current draw or resulting pack-temperature response within the diagnostic window. The most common physical causes are a blown fuse or failed fan-motor relay, a fan motor bearing that has seized or is drawing excess current, and debris lodged in the fan shroud restricting the blade. This fault is specific to air-cooled pack platforms and has no coolant-loop component to bleed or top off, unlike the liquid-cooled pack's coolant pump.

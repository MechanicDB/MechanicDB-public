ABS Hydraulic Inlet Outlet Valve Circuit

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code C0010 (Left Front Inlet Control) sets when the ABS module commands a corner's hydraulic inlet or outlet solenoid to hold, build, or release brake pressure and the valve fails to respond, releases pressure for longer than the calibrated window, or the module cannot verify the valve's calibration or supply voltage. Root causes include a seized or debris-contaminated valve poppet, an open or shorted valve coil, or corrosion at the HCU connector. Any diagnostic or repair on the hydraulic block requires a scan tool ABS bleed procedure afterward because trapped air cannot be purged by gravity or manual bleeding alone.

Cylinder Knock Threshold Exceeded

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
P2336 (Cylinder 1 Above Knock/Combustion Vibration Sensor Threshold) records that the knock sensing system measured combustion vibration above the protection threshold in one specific cylinder — real detonation, not a sensor circuit fault. Carbon accumulation raising that cylinder's effective compression, a lean condition from a restricted injector on that hole, fuel of lower octane than the engine requires, and an overheating spot in the cooling jacket around that cylinder are the physical causes worth checking in order.

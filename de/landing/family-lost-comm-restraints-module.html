Lost Comm Restraints Module

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
U0151 means a module lost contact with the restraints control module, an impact sensor, seat belt pretensioner, or a similar SRS-network node. This bus is treated as its own protected safety segment because a wiring fault here can mask a genuine airbag readiness problem, so diagnosis must include a full occupant-restraint system self-test rather than assuming a simple wiring repair clears the underlying safety concern. Before disconnecting any restraints connector, the battery must be disconnected and left to sit through the manufacturer's capacitor-discharge wait time, since backup power can fire a pretensioner or airbag during service.

Lost Comm HVAC Module

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
U010F (Lost Communication With Air Conditioning Control Module) covers loss of contact with the HVAC control head or one of the small LIN-networked blend-door, mode, and refrigerant sensor actuators behind the dash. These actuators cycle constantly with every temperature and mode change and their multi-pin connectors sit directly behind the defroster ducting, so repeated heat/cool cycling and vibration from the actuator motors themselves -- not road exposure or engine heat -- is the dominant wear mechanism. A failed actuator motor that also carries the LIN transceiver is a more common root cause here than in most other module classes, since the sensor and actuator functions share one small circuit board.

Diese 29 Diagnose-Fehlercodes teilen einen Erkennungsmechanismus und einen Reparaturweg. Deshalb fasst MechanicDB sie in einer einzigen, redaktionell erstellten Fehlerfamilie zusammen, statt dieselbe Erklärung 29-mal zu wiederholen.

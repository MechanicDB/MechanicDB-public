Vehicle Dynamics Sensor Circuit

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code C0061 (Lateral Acceleration Sensor) sets when the stability control module cannot get a valid or correlated reading from one or more of the inertial measurement sensors that report the vehicle's yaw rate, lateral acceleration, longitudinal acceleration, roll rate, or vertical acceleration -- the circuit is open, missing calibration, intermittent, or one axis disagrees with a related channel. These sensors are typically packaged together in a single multi-axis module mounted near the vehicle's center of gravity, so a connector or mounting fault at that module tends to affect several axes at once. Physical causes include a loose or corroded module connector, a module knocked out of alignment by an impact, or an internal MEMS sensor failure.

Motor Generator Overtemp Shutdown

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code P0A2F sets when the drive motor / generator inverter thermal protection reaches a temperature high enough that the control module derates or shuts down output to prevent damage. The most common physical causes are a low motor electronics coolant level or a restricted coolant flow path, a failing coolant pump, and sustained high-load operation (towing, repeated hard acceleration) that outpaces the cooling system's capacity.

Lost Comm EV Charging Module

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
U0110 means a module on the high-voltage traction network -- a battery interface module, drive motor inverter, DC/DC or onboard charger control module -- has dropped off its dedicated network segment routed alongside the high-voltage cabling and isolated from the 12V chassis ground system. A failed isolation barrier or a loose high-voltage service disconnect can take down communication even when the low-voltage signal wiring itself is intact. Work here requires high-voltage safety training and insulated tools, since tracing the fault may involve components carrying several hundred volts DC.

Diese 66 Diagnose-Fehlercodes teilen einen Erkennungsmechanismus und einen Reparaturweg. Deshalb fasst MechanicDB sie in einer einzigen, redaktionell erstellten Fehlerfamilie zusammen, statt dieselbe Erklärung 66-mal zu wiederholen.

SRS Impact Rollover Sensor Circuit

Diagnose & technische Erläuterung
Code B0090 (Left Frontal Restraints Sensor) sets when the SRS module loses a valid signal from one of the satellite impact sensors mounted in the doors, pillars, or frontal crush zones, or from the rollover sensor, all of which feed the crash-severity algorithm that decides which restraints to deploy and how aggressively. A circuit fault here means the module cannot fully evaluate a future crash event at that location even though no crash has occurred, which is why these codes command the SRS warning lamp on immediately. Common causes are a corroded connector at a door- or pillar-mounted sensor exposed to moisture, a chafed harness at a door hinge or sill, or physical damage to the sensor from a prior minor collision that was never inspected.

Diese 16 Diagnose-Fehlercodes teilen einen Erkennungsmechanismus und einen Reparaturweg. Deshalb fasst MechanicDB sie in einer einzigen, redaktionell erstellten Fehlerfamilie zusammen, statt dieselbe Erklärung 16-mal zu wiederholen.
